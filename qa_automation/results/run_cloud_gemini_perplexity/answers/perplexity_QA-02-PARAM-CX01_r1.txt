PROMPT:
What is the OID of hrSystemUptime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
OID: 1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1 (HOST-RESOURCES-MIB::hrSystemUptime). For an SNMP GET, use the scalar instance OID 1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1.0.
net-snmp
+1
Unit: TimeTicks, measured in hundredths of a second (10 ms per tick). Divide the returned value by 100 to get seconds; for example, 12345 means 123.45 seconds.
snmp-monitoring
It measures time since the host was last initialized, unlike sysUpTime, which measures uptime of the network-management portion of the system.
