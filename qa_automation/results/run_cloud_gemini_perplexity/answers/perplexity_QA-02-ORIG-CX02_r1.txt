PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The OID of sysUpTime is 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3. To retrieve its scalar value with an SNMP GET, use sysUpTime.0, or 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0.
