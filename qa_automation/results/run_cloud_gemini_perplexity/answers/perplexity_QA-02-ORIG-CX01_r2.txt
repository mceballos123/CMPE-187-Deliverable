PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
OID: 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3 for sysUpTime; use 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0 (sysUpTime.0) when querying its scalar instance.
