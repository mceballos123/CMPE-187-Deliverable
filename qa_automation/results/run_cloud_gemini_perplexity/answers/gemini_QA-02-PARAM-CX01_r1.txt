PROMPT:
What is the OID of hrSystemUptime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The OID of hrSystemUptime (from HOST-RESOURCES-MIB) is 1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1 (polled as scalar instance 1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1.0).
   Observium MIB Browser
Its syntax is TimeTicks, meaning its raw value is measured in hundredths of a second (centiseconds, or 0.01 seconds) since the host was last initialized.
Practical Notes
Host vs. SNMP uptime: Unlike sysUpTime (1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0), which measures uptime of the network management portion of the system, hrSystemUptime measures how long the host itself has been running since initialization.
   Observium MIB Browser
32-bit rollover: Because TimeTicks is a 32-bit unsigned counter in hundredths of a second, it wraps around roughly every 497.1 days (2^32 / 100 / 86400 seconds).
