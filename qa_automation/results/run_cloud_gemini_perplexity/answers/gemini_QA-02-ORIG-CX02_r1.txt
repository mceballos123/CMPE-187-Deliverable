PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The OID of sysUpTime is 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3 (with scalar instance 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0 when queried directly), and its value uses hundredths of a second (centiseconds), represented as an SNMP TimeTicks value.
   MyF5
Practical Details to Keep in Mind
What it actually measures: sysUpTime measures the time since the network management portion of the system (such as the SNMP agent/subsystem) was last initialized—not necessarily full hardware/OS boot time.
 Restarting the SNMP service can reset it to 0. For host/OS uptime, hrSystemUptime (1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1.0) is often used instead.
