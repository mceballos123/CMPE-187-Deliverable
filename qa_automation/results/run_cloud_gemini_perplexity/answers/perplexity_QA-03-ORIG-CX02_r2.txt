PROMPT:
A 32-bit ifInOctets counter on a router interface reads 4,294,000,000 at the first poll and 1,000,000 at the second poll 60 seconds later. Assuming the counter wrapped once, what was the average inbound throughput in bits per second?

ANSWER:
A 32-bit counter wraps at
2
32
=4,294,967,296. The increase was
4,294,967,296−4,294,000,000+1,000,000=1,967,296 octets. Over 60 seconds, that is
(1,967,296×8)/60≈262,306 bits per second (about 262.3 kbps).
