PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The OID of sysUpTime is 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3 (queried as 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0 for the scalar instance).
   oidref.com
Its value has syntax TimeTicks and uses hundredths of a second (centiseconds, i.e., 
1 tick=0.01 seconds).
A Couple of Practical Details
What it actually measures: sysUpTime tracks the time since the network management portion of the system (the SNMP agent) was last initialized—not necessarily the full OS or hardware boot time.
 Restarting the SNMP daemon resets sysUpTime to 0. (If you want host/OS uptime, hrSystemUptime at 1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1.0 is often used instead.)
