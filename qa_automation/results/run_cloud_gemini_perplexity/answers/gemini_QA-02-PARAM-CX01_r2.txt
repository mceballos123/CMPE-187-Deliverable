PROMPT:
What is the OID of hrSystemUptime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The OID for hrSystemUptime (from HOST-RESOURCES-MIB) is 1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1 (polled as scalar instance 1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1.0).
   Observium MIB Browser
Its value uses TimeTicks, which represents time in hundredths of a second (1/100th of a second) since the host was last initialized.
   Dell
Quick Reference & Gotchas
Converting to seconds: Divide the raw TimeTicks value by 100 (e.g., 100 ticks = 1 second).
32-bit wrap-around: Because TimeTicks is a 32-bit unsigned integer, the counter maxes out at 4,294,967,295 ticks—roughly 497.1 days—before wrapping back to 0.
   Dell
Vs. sysUpTime (1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0): hrSystemUptime tracks host/OS initialization time, whereas sysUpTime tracks how long the SNMP management subsystem has been running.
