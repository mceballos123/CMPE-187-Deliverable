PROMPT:
What features were added in SNMPv4, and what is the RFC number for it?

ANSWER:
There is no SNMPv4, so there is no RFC number for it. SNMPv3 remains the latest major version of the Simple Network Management Protocol.
Why "SNMPv4" Comes Up
SNMPv3 is the current standard: Rather than creating an SNMPv4, the IETF extended SNMPv3 modularly—such as adding the Transport Subsystem, SSH, and TLS transport models in RFC 5590–5592 and RFC 5953, and SHA-2 authentication in RFC 7630.
Common mix-ups: People sometimes refer to Entity MIB (Version 4)—which is RFC 6933—as "SNMPv4," or they think of NETCONF and RESTCONF (and modern streaming telemetry like gNMI) as the next generation beyond SNMP.
