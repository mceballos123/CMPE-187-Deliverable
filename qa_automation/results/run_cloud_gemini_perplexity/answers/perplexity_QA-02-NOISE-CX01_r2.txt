PROMPT:
what is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its vaule use

ANSWER:
OID: 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3 (SNMPv2-MIB::sysUpTime). When querying its scalar value, use 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0 (sysUpTime.0).
mibs.observium
Unit: TimeTicks, measured in hundredths of a second—one tick is 0.01 seconds, so divide the raw value by 100 to get seconds. For example, 12345 means 123.45 seconds.
arista.site
It measures time since the network-management portion of the device was last initialized, not necessarily time since the device itself booted; restarting the SNMP agent can reset it.
