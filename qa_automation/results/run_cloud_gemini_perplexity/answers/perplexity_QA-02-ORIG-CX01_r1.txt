PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The sysUpTime object OID is 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3. For an SNMP GET, use its scalar instance, sysUpTime.0: 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0.
