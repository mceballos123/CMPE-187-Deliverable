PROMPT:
What is the OID of hrSystemUptime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The OID of hrSystemUptime is:
1.3.6.1.2.1.25.1.1
Unit: TimeTicks, measured in hundredths of a second (1/100 second).
1 TimeTick = 0.01 seconds (10 milliseconds).
100 TimeTicks = 1 second.
It measures the time elapsed since the host was last initialized (rebooted).
MIB: HOST-RESOURCES-MIB, defined in RFC 2790.
