PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The SNMP sysUpTime OID is:
OID
1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0
Unit: TimeTicks (hundredths of a second)
1 TimeTick = 0.01 seconds
100 TimeTicks = 1 second
What does sysUpTime measure?
It measures the time since the network management portion of the device was last initialized (typically when the SNMP agent or device started).
Example
If an SNMP query returns:
text
sysUpTime.0 = 12345678
Convert it to seconds:
12
12,345,678÷100=123,456.78
That's approximately 34.29 hours.
Important interview point
sysUpTime OID: 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3
sysUpTime.0 is the instance OID used to query the scalar value.
Data type: TimeTicks
Unit: 1/100th of a second.
Standard reference:
RFC 3418 – SNMP MIB
