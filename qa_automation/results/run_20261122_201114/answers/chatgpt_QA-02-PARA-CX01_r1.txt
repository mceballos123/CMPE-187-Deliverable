PROMPT:
Give me the object identifier for sysUpTime and tell me what unit its value is measured in.

ANSWER:
The SNMP object identifier (OID) for sysUpTime is:
1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0
Unit: Hundredths of a second (0.01 seconds).
Data type: TimeTicks.
Meaning: Time elapsed since the network management portion of the system was last initialized.
Example: A value of 10000 represents 100 seconds (or 1 minute and 40 seconds).
Source:
RFC 3418 – SNMP Management Information Base 
.
