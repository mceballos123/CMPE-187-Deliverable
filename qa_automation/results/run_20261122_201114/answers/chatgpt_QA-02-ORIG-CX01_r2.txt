PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The SNMP OID for sysUpTime is:
1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3
Unit: Hundredths of a second (1/100 second).
Data type: TimeTicks.
Meaning: Time elapsed since the network management subsystem was last initialized.
For example, a value of 10000 represents 100 seconds.
Source:
RFC 3418 – SNMP MIB 
.
