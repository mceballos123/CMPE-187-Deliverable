PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The SNMP OID for sysUpTime is:
1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0
Its value is measured in hundredths of a second (10 milliseconds), also known as TimeTicks.
For example, a value of 1000 represents 10 seconds.
Note: The .0 suffix identifies the scalar instance of the OID.
Source:
RFC 3418 – SNMP MIB specification
