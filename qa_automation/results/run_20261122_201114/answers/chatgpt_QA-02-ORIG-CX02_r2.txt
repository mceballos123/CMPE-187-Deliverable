PROMPT:
What is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its value use?

ANSWER:
The SNMP OID for sysUpTime is:
FULL OID (INSTANCE)
1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0
MIB: SNMPv2-MIB
Data type: TimeTicks
Unit: Hundredths of a second (1/100 second)
Access: Read-only
How is the value calculated?
Every 1 TimeTick = 0.01 seconds.
For example, if sysUpTime returns 123456789:
123456789÷100=1,234,567.89 seconds
That's approximately 14.29 days.
Important detail
sysUpTime measures the time since the device's network management subsystem was last initialized, not necessarily the time since the entire device booted.
