PROMPT:
what is the OID of sysUpTime, and what unit does its vaule use

ANSWER:
The SNMP sysUpTime object has the following properties:
OID: 1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3
Data type: TimeTicks (32-bit unsigned integer)
Unit: Hundredths of a second (1/100 second).
Meaning: Time elapsed since the network management portion of the device was last initialized.
Example:
If sysUpTime returns 123456:
123456÷100=1234.56 seconds
That's approximately 20 minutes and 34.56 seconds.
Note: When querying the scalar instance through SNMP, append .0 to the OID:
1.3.6.1.2.1.1.3.0
Source:
RFC 3418 – SNMP MIB
